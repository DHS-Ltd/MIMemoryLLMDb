## What BDC is

A wholly-owned but separately-modeled diagnostic centre. It earns revenue from diagnostic tests and
doctor chambers, and simultaneously serves as **DHS's proving ground** — the place DHS dogfoods its
HMS software and builds the reference model it will sell to other centres.

BDC plays three roles at once:
1. **Operating diagnostic centre** — tests, doctor chambers, report delivery.
2. **Patient-generation engine** — marketing + funnel (see `../programs/bdc-patient-generation.md`).
3. **Proving ground** — runs HMS (MIMP-003); validates the software before external sale.

## Core USP

> "You don't need to come if you can't come" — remote video appointment + online doctor counselling
> through the (planned) BDC website, for patients who cannot travel to a chamber.

## Marketing & lead channels

- **Miking** (loudspeaker announcements) — Belabo, Dulalkandi, Morjal; doctor-based / leaflet distribution.
- **Pharmacy liaison** — per-patient commission (~200 BDT noted).
- **Local agents** — ~6 employed; patient acquisition.
- **PC (prescription) doctors** — referral channel; currently under-converting (commission/trust gap noted).
- **Facebook** — primary online channel; see the marketing program for the full audit.
- **Proposed new channels** — online traffic, tele-marketing infrastructure, home sample collection.
