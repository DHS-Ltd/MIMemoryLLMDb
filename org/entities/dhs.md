## What DHS is

The parent healthcare company. Supplies medical equipment and healthcare technology to hospitals
and diagnostic centres, and builds software for the same market. Positioning is partnership, trust,
and tailored value — explicitly not market domination. See `../business.md` for mission/vision/USP.

## Activities

### Software SaaS
- **PACS product** — DICOM viewer (OHIF fork, MIMP-005) + marketing/commercial (MIMP-004); sold to
  external hospitals. **Current focus: first external sale to Ibn Sina by July 2026** (see `../north-star.md`).
- **HMS product** — hospital/diagnostic management (MIMP-003); currently runs BDC (dogfooding) and is
  the productization candidate for external centres.
- **Internal tooling** — ImageConverter (MIMP-001), MIMemoryLLMDb/mimp (MIMP-002).

### Diagnostic-Centre business
Operated through the wholly-owned subsidiary **BDC** (see `bdc.md`). Current revenue + proving ground
+ lead generation.

### Equipment supply
The long-term revenue anchor: expensive equipment, very long sales cycles, backed by strong global
manufacturer connections and 25+ years of experience. Currently groundwork — fed by the trust and
awareness generated by SaaS + BDC. See `../programs/equipment-deal-pipeline.md`.

## Strategic thesis

Land with software + diagnostic-centre work → earn trust/awareness → convert to high-ticket equipment
deals. Every move is weighed for both direct revenue and trust compounding toward equipment.

## External references (not entities)

- **Ibn Sina** — intended first paying PACS customer AND the master proof point for subsequent PACS
  sales. Tracked here as a reference/target, not as a brain entity.
