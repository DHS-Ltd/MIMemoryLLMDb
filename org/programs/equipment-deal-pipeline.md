# Program: Equipment-Deal Pipeline

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

## Why this is the destination, not the start

Equipment supply is DHS's **long-term core target** and intended primary revenue anchor: very
expensive equipment, very long sales cycles, backed by 25+ years of experience and strong global
manufacturer connections. It is deliberately *not* pushed yet — it is fed by the trust and market
awareness generated by the Software-SaaS and Diagnostic-Centre (BDC) work.

## How the flywheel feeds it

SaaS + BDC build credibility with hospital owners and doctors → those relationships and the earned
trust become qualified leads for equipment → DHS is trusted to source the *right* equipment for them.

## Current state (as of 2026-06-02)

- No active named equipment deal recorded yet. **Verify** whether any early leads exist.
- Primary near-term contribution to this pipeline is *indirect*: every SaaS win (e.g. the Ibn Sina
  PACS sale) and BDC success compounds the trust this pipeline draws on.
