## The intended funnel ("you don't need to come if you can't come")

1. Patient fills a form (or agent submits via Google Form) to request a video appointment.
2. Coordinator confirms by phone, marks confirmed.
3. Auto-generated Google Meet link at appointment time; WhatsApp notification to patient + coordinator.
4. Doctor counsels the patient; patient pays for the prescription.
5. Doctor drives an in-person visit / diagnostics where needed.

Supporting infrastructure planned: BDC website (simple interface + patient DB), doctor database
(name, speciality, picture, availability), Google-Forms→DB integration, and lead→converted-patient
tracking. **The complete system should be in native Bengali.**
