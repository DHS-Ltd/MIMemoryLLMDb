## Abstract

Records why the patient-ownership category was abandoned: the category had no pull (market research shows low patient expectation), field data was thin (Cumilla ~1.4 referring doctors per centre, 56 enrolments across 5 centres vs ~1,257 at Ibn Sina where forwarding is automatic), and a stronger unserved need existed. Also rejects doctor-first go-to-market as the primary motion in favour of centre-first with catchment compounding.
