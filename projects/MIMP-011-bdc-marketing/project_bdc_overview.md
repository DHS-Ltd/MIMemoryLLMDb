## Baroicha Diagnostic Center (BDC)

**Bengali name:** বারৈচা ডায়াগনস্টিক সেন্টার  
**Location:** মুজিবর শপিং কমপ্লেক্স, বেলাবো, নরসিংদী, Bangladesh  
**Phone:** 01913-836244  
**Google account:** directhospitalsolutionsltd@gmail.com

A diagnostic center in Belabo Upazila (sub-district), Narsingdi district. Serves rural Bangladeshi patients. Primary competitors are city-based centers in Narsingdi town and Bhairab — BDC's advantage is proximity, price transparency, and local trust.

**Why:** Build patient volume and trust in a community where healthcare access is low and people default to Dhaka or Narsingdi for diagnostics.

**How to apply:** All patient-facing content must be in Bengali. Never use English in captions or posts. Reference local village names to create community resonance.

## Brand

- **Primary color:** `#003344` (deep teal)
- **Accent:** `#00897B` (accent mint) — non-negotiable pair, never use separately
- **Language:** Bangla first for all patient-facing copy
- **Tone:** "Knowledgeable neighbour" — warm, local, never corporate
- **Logo:** Use Bangla logo variant for print materials
- **Font:** Noto Sans Bengali for all Bengali text in images

| 5 | Social Proof | সামাজিক প্রমাণ | 10% |

**Philosophy:** Value 70% / Existence reminder 30%. Name BDC AFTER delivering value. Every post ends with a soft CTA and phone number.

## 60-Day Content Calendar (Apr–Jun 2026)

**Period:** 26 April 2026 → 24 June 2026  
**Frequency:** 4–5 posts/week  
**Best posting times:** 8–9:30 AM, 1–2 PM, 8–9:30 PM (UTC+6 Bangladesh time)

**Critical issue found in audit:** Posts were going out at 3–4 AM BST due to timezone misconfiguration. Posting timezone must be set to UTC+6 (Asia/Dhaka).

5 phases:
1. Foundation (Days 1–14): Establish voice
2. Education (Days 15–28): Trusted health resource
3. Service Launch (Days 29–42): Convert awareness to appointments
4. Community & Proof (Days 43–56): Deepen trust
5. Retention (Days 57–60): Lock in repeat behavior

## Lead Magnets (Hormozi Methodology)

4 lead magnets targeting rural Bangladeshi healthcare audience:

| # | Lead Magnet | Trigger | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM1 | 5-Minute WhatsApp Risk Score | "চেক" keyword → auto-reply | Weeks 4–6 |
| LM2 | Free Report Explanation | Photo of report → WhatsApp | Weeks 1–3 (first to launch) |
| LM3 | Pregnancy Monthly Guide + Free USG Voucher | Join WhatsApp group | Weeks 7–9 |
| LM4 | Monthly Free BP + Sugar Check Day | Community health camp | Week 10+ |

**Primary conversion channel:** WhatsApp Business (not Facebook Messenger, not phone call)

## Facebook Page Audit (as of 23 May 2026)

- **232 posts** analyzed (Nov 2024 – May 2026)
- **Core diagnosis:** Consistent posting without a funnel. No lead capture launched. KPI tracker never filled in.
- **Top performers:** Village-naming posts (avg 31.3 reactions), equipment explainers, doctor profiles
- **Critical gaps:** ACS virtual consultation not built, health camp not confirmed, all 16 KPI metrics blank
- **Execution rate:** 18% exact plan match, 64% off-plan posts, 18% skipped

**7 priority recommendations** were issued — fix timezone, launch LM#2, update KPI tracker, confirm health camp date.

## Audience Segments

1. Farmers and day-laborers (need quick, cheap diagnostics near home)
2. Pregnant mothers (USG, antenatal package)
3. Middle-aged patients with existing diagnoses (diabetes, hypertension follow-up)
4. Adult children managing elderly parents' health from Dhaka
