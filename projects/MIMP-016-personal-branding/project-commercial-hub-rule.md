Established 2026-08-03 via `/grill-with-docs`, run from DHS-PACS. DH Solutions Ltd centralized all
Commercial Content (marketing plans, positioning, pricing, official channel posts, pitch decks,
customer proposals) for its three products — DH PACS, DHV, DHDicomAnalyzerPro — into DHS-PACS,
regardless of which repo that content is drafted in. Full reasoning: `E:\DHS-PACS\CONTEXT-MAP.md`
and its ADR-0001.

**Relevance to this repo:** if a session here produces anything in that category — even
incidentally while drafting a personal post — it does not stay here. See this repo's
`CLAUDE.md` (new this session) and the **Commercial Hub** term in `CONTEXT.md`, which sits next
to (but is distinct from) the existing **Brand Separation** term: Brand Separation governs what a
personal post is allowed to *say* (never name the product); Commercial Hub governs where
commercial *files* are allowed to *live* (never here).

**Why this doesn't conflict with [[project-overview]] or [[project-dhs-pacs-relationship]]:** this
repo's own content (personal LinkedIn voice, calendar, CV) is unaffected — it's the opposite flow.
This rule only stops company-commercial material from accumulating here by accident.
