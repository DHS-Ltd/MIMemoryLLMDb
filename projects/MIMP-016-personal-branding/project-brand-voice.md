**What Maidul is NOT:** A vendor. A consultant. A teleradiology operator. A generic digital health advocate.

**Audience — surgeon-first (changed 2026-08-05):** Primary reader is the surgeon. Two tracks: **ortho/trauma for reach** (largest BD surgeon population), **vascular/neuro/maxillofacial for depth** (sharpest unmet need, smaller audience). Hospital directors remain readers but are no longer the target. The radiologist is a deliberate non-persona.

**The five pillars (P5 added 2026-08-05, is the spine — ~45% of content):**
- P1 Medical Imaging Demystified · P2 Bangladesh Healthcare IT · P3 Patient Perspective (Bengali/bilingual) · P4 Inside the Machine Room · **P5 Advanced Post-Processing** — segmentation, 3D reconstruction, centerline, stenosis quantification, biomodeling/STL, and documented failures, built from the ~549-image Inobitec corpus.

**Writing rhythm:**
- Mechanism first, then what it means
- Long explanatory sentence → short declarative payoff
- Opener: specific number, scene, or credential moment — never context/background
- Close: one short reframe sentence — never a CTA, never a question
- 2–4 sentences per paragraph; white space matters

**The 9 litmus tests (run before every post — expanded from 7 for P5):**
1. Does the post open with a specific number, scene, or credential — not with context?
2. Does the reader know something real at the end they didn't know at the start?
3. Is DH PACS / DH Solutions Ltd / any product completely absent?
4. If a number appears — is it exact and sourced?
5. If about patient experience — is it Bengali or bilingual?
6. Is the closing line a reframe, not a question and not a CTA?
7. Zero exclamation marks, zero "excited to share," zero bait hooks?
8. **Has every image cleared the PHI Gate** — masked, cropped, renamed, contact-sheet verified?
9. **Is the engine unnamed**, and is the subject the technique rather than the tool?

**Language rule:**
- Default: English (technology, infrastructure, systems, data)
- Bengali or bilingual: Pillar 3 only (patient experience, personal story)
- Test: system → English; human experience → Bengali/bilingual

**Key hard lines (non-negotiable):**
- Never name DH PACS, DH Solutions Ltd, DHV, or DHDicomAnalyzerPro in personal posts
- **Never name the post-processing engine either** — always "the post-processing software"; if asked, use the fixed reply: "This is a commercial post-processing suite — not something I built. The technique is what matters, and it works the same way in any capable workstation."
- No exclamation marks. No "excited to share." No "game-changer." No "revolutionize."
- No polls, no reposts, no external links in post body (links in first comment)
- ৳14.6B figure always sourced: NBR import data, HS code 3701.10
- HIPAA always "built to HIPAA standards" — never "HIPAA certified"
- No mention of BDC diagnostic center or fitness tracker project
- No per-patient pricing in any post
- Every P5 image passes the PHI Gate (mask → crop → rename → contact-sheet verify) before use

**Why:** Original voice established 2026-06-24 to 2026-06-29; audience and P5 added 2026-08-05 when `DHS_BRAND_STRATEGY.md` reorganised DH Solutions around the Surgeon Chain and demoted patient ownership from headline claim to mechanism. The personal brand must build trust independently — DH PACS benefits downstream as a consequence, never as the explicit goal of any post.

**How to apply:** Read the full profile before drafting any LinkedIn post, comment, outreach message, or YouTube script where Maidul is the author. Run all 9 litmus tests as hard pass/fail gates. If any answer is no, rewrite before publishing.
