cyclotron), the user runs a **second, unrelated revenue line** in this same project directory:
a PPP pitch to **Bangladesh Medical University (BMU)** — formerly BSMMU, renamed post-2024 —
who already own a working IBA cyclotron but suffer from an inadequate local consumable/reagent
supply chain. The user's company (acting as "the Operator" in this report — see
[[reports-evidence-discipline]] for why the entity name is anonymized) proposes to supply
reagents/consumables and staff to run the facility 5 days/week instead of 2, splitting profit
60:40 (BMU:Operator). Context and decisions live in
`BMU_Cyclotron_proposal/CONTEXT.md` (see root `CONTEXT-MAP.md` for the split).

is a supply/operations play, not a hardware sale. Same corporate umbrella (also a Sumitomo
distributor + tied up with major radiopharma manufacturers) but distinct commercial logic.
**How to apply:** when working in `BMU_Cyclotron_proposal/`, use "the Operator" and "BMU" (never
the real company name or "BSMMU") per that context's decided terminology. The first deliverable
is a 2-page champion-enablement concept note (`BMU_PPP_Concept_Note.md` + `.html`, no PDF,
A4 print styling) aimed at an internal BMU contact who will carry it upward — not a final
contractual proposal.
