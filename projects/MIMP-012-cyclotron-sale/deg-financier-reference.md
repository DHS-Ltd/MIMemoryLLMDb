**DEG — Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft mbH**, Kämmergasse 22, 50676 Cologne.
Germany's development finance institution, **wholly owned by KfW**, founded 1962. The financier
approached for the TMSS cyclotron project — see [[tmss-cyclotron-sale]] for the strategy built on
these facts. Researched 2026-09-14; re-verify before quoting in a document, DFI terms drift.

Website: https://www.deginvest.de/ · Corporate solutions page carries the product parameters.

## What they can write a cheque for

| Instrument | Parameters |
|---|---|
| Long-term loan | **Minimum USD 6M**, tenor **4–12 years**, EUR / USD / selected local currency |
| Equity | **Minimum USD 6M**, usually a minority stake with board representation |
| Mezzanine | Hybrid, risk-adjusted return |
| Guarantees, bonds | Also offered |
| **develoPPP** | Up to 50% of project costs, capped **EUR 2M** — pilot / environmental / qualification |
| **Business Support Services** | Up to 50% of consulting costs, capped **EUR 200,000** |
| ImpactConnect | Loans up to EUR 5–10M, explicitly **for European companies** investing in emerging markets |

Collateral on market practice, preferably the company's assets in-country. Eligibility follows the
**OECD DAC** list (Bangladesh qualifies). Healthcare is an eligible sector. **2X Global** gender-lens
criteria explicitly encouraged.

**The mandate constraint that shapes everything:** DEG finances **private enterprise**. KfW does the
government-to-government half. An NGO borrower is a structuring problem, not an automatic no — but it
has to be pre-empted, which is why the TMSS proposal recommends a ring-fenced SPV.

**The small windows may not be reachable by MED-I.** develoPPP and ImpactConnect appear aimed at
German and European companies. **MED-I is Thai.** Unconfirmed. Always phrase as a question to DEG,
never as an assumption.

## Bangladesh footprint

- Active since **1981**: **41 companies, EUR 515M** in equity and long-term loans.
- **Founding shareholder of IPDC and IDLC** — two of the country's best-known financial institutions
  exist partly because of DEG. Strong ground for asking their view on a borrowing structure.
- **"German Desk" at Mutual Trust Bank (MTB)**, Dhaka, opened 17 April 2018; relationship manager
  Emdadul Karim Quoreshi. **Probably the "liaison bank" the user referred to — inferred, unconfirmed.**
- Local representation via the **KfW Office, Dhaka**.
- Benchmark ticket: recent senior secured loan of **USD 25M** to Banga Building Materials. So a
  **USD 20M anchor ask is real but unexceptional** for them.

## Bangladesh cancer facts gathered alongside (GLOBOCAN 2022 / IARC, IAEA sector reporting)

- **167,256** new cancer cases/yr; **116,598** deaths. Forecast **348,000 cases by 2050**.
- Leading incidence per 100,000: oesophagus 16.0, **breast 15.2**, **cervix uteri 11.3**, lip/oral
  10.3, lung 8.8. Bangladesh ranks **first globally** for oesophageal cancer.
- ~**15 PET/CT scanners** nationally; routine cyclotron production effectively confined to Dhaka
  (NINMAS/BSMMU plus one private machine running twice weekly). Chattogram in progress. **Nothing in
  the north.**
