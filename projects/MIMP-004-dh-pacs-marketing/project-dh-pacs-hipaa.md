DHS/DH PACS is actively working toward HIPAA compliance as a product requirement.

**Why:** Bangladesh hospital decision-makers know HIPAA and treat it as the gold standard for healthcare data security, even though Bangladeshi law does not mandate it. It is the benchmark they aspire to, not one they currently meet. DHS wants to meet it too.

**Current state:** DHS is not yet fully HIPAA-certified as a company, but the open-source technology stack underlying DH PACS (OHIF viewer, Tailscale VPN, etc.) has individually passed HIPAA compliance requirements. The compliance gap is at the company/process level, not the technology level.

**How to apply:** Always include HIPAA in website copy and product messaging — it is the right and expected reference for this audience. Simultaneously, treat full HIPAA compliance as a non-negotiable product roadmap item. When writing content, phrase it as "HIPAA-standard" or "built to HIPAA standards" (accurate given the stack) rather than claiming the company itself is certified (not yet accurate). When asked to plan the product or suggest features, flag any HIPAA gap as a priority item.
