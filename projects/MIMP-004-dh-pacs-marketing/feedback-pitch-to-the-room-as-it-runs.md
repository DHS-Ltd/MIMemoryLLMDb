Pitch only the benefits a customer can use in their current setup — never a technically better
workflow their room cannot run. Before recommending how staff will use a product, ask who does each
step today (e.g. the Report Typist at Ibn Sina 15/A, who pre-loads the session before the
radiologist arrives).

**Why:** 2026-10-23, Ibn Sina 15/A Local PACS pitch grilling — Claude recommended radiologists read
directly from the archive on technical merit (one hop, compressed on the wire). The user rejected it:
the typist pre-loads each session onto the workstation, so direct reading was "some fictional benefit
which they are unable to use in their current setup".

**How to apply:** in any pitch or product-workflow question, first map the real people and steps in
the room (ask the user — domain knowledge is theirs), then design the benefit around that. Technical
superiority is a secondary argument. See [[project-ibnsina-15a-local-pacs-pitch]].
