**Format pivot (2026-07-11, confirmed by user, applies going forward)**: chapter reports are no longer "study guide + checklist" — they are **demo playbooks**. Each chapter's report leads with 2-3 **flagship demos** (not exhaustive section-by-section coverage), each demo spec'd as: data to pull → numbered click-by-click steps → the specific "wow moment" → an exact line of narration to say to a clinician at that moment → fallback/troubleshooting. Objective stated by user: "reproduce each of the video with my own patient study in my own machine... to demonstrate on the fly to the interested medical person." A shorter "Toolbox reference" section (checklist style, like the old format) covers supporting mechanics that aren't demo-worthy alone. Sections gated on a not-yet-covered chapter (e.g. Ch3 §3.16/§3.17.3 needing Ch4 Series Fusion) get a "Deferred" callout instead of a full writeup.

**Video cross-referencing** ([[inobitec-scope-loop]]'s Feature-Insight docs under `VIdeo_Insights\`) is mandatory for Ch3 (done), Ch5 MPR, and Ch6 Segmentation — each flagship demo should link the specific video report it's reproducing.

Recurring findings logged across chapters, worth knowing before touching this data again: derived/exported results (fusion, subtraction, stitching, reslice, bone-removal) always land as a **new series** on the panel, never a live view update; only an MPR/3D tab opened *from inside* a fusion/segmentation tab carries full multi-layer context (a separately-opened tab looks identical but silently disables layer-dependent exports); bone-kernel (sharp) reconstructions trace/segment noticeably better than soft-kernel; study labels can be flat-out wrong (E1026604 was labeled "Head" but every series was abdomen), not just incomplete — always sanity-check actual series content before committing prep time.
