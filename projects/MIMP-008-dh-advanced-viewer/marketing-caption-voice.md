Rules for writing captions/overlays on [[client-facing-clips-pipeline]] assets:

- **Never write "Inobitec DICOM Viewer Pro" or "DICOM Viewer Pro" as a proper-noun brand
  name in captions.** The user found it awkward on the ear. Call it **"the post-processing
  software"** instead. (Generic category language like "modern DICOM viewing" is fine —
  it's the specific product-name phrasing that's banned, not the word DICOM itself.)
  **Why:** direct correction on the first drafted caption set.

- **Name the real hospital/source when known** (e.g. "an Abdomen case from IBN Sina
  Hospital, Dhanmondi, Dhaka") — the user wants real-case credibility stated explicitly,
  not genericized. **How to apply:** ask which hospital/case if not already established in
  the conversation; don't default to anonymous phrasing.

- **The core sales angle is time savings for time-strapped technologists**, not diagnostic
  accuracy or feature breadth. The user's own framing: "technologists who do this job
  don't have much time." **How to apply:** wherever a clip's actual elapsed time supports
  it, state a concrete, conservative time figure (e.g. "under a minute", "under 90
  seconds") near the end, and land the final line on time-back-for-the-technologist rather
  than a generic "try our software" CTA. Never make diagnostic-quality or clinical-accuracy
  claims — stick to speed/ease claims only.

- **PHI caution (flagged, not yet resolved as of 2026-07-21):** raw footage opens on a real
  patient list screen (Name/ID/Sex/DOB columns). Once a caption names the real hospital,
  that screen becomes a bigger leak risk if legible at full resolution. Flag this every
  time a new Client-Facing Clip's opening frames show a patient browser/list — recommend
  blurring/cropping that moment before external distribution — but it's the user's call,
  not a blocker.
