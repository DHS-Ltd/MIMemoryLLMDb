When presenting decisions, **lead with a single clear recommendation** and let the user
confirm or redirect — they engage well with structured choices that mark the recommended
option, not open-ended questions.

The user explicitly wants **detailed visual design handled in a dedicated design session**
(using the design workflow/skill), separate from planning. Don't try to finalize look-and-feel
during strategy work — agree the direction ("evolve the identity"), then defer specifics.

**Why:** the user is non-technical and time-conscious; a recommendation to react to is faster
and clearer for them than a blank menu, and they treat design as its own reviewable milestone.

**How to apply:** in planning/grilling, always propose a default + rationale. Keep visual
design out of scope until a design session is explicitly started. See [[user-medi-thailand-owner]].
