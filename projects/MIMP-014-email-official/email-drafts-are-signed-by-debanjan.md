When Maidul asks for an email to the SMCH Chairman, he is drafting on behalf of **Debanjan
Hazra** (`debanjan.hazra@adglobal.eu`), who signs and sends it. First person in Maidul's
instructions — "I spoke to him Saturday", "I asked what the next step was" — refers to
Debanjan, not Maidul. Maidul is a silent CC on the thread.

**Why:** assuming Maidul was the speaker produces a draft in the wrong voice and, worse,
mis-attributes who was on a call — which matters when the email's purpose is to put a verbal
conversation on the record.

**How to apply:** default to Debanjan as author and signatory for anything going to the
Chairman, in Bengali, closing *বিনীত, দেবাঞ্জন হাজরা*. Confirm only if the instruction
suggests otherwise. Part of [[brb-smch-cyclotron-deal]].
