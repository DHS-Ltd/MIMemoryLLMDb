Med I (Thailand) is selling a Sumitomo cyclotron system to Selima Medical College Hospital
Ltd. (Kushtia, a BRB group concern) against signed PO SMCH/MEDI/26-002 of 2 March 2026.
Chairman Md. Mozibar Rahman is the sole decision-maker. Terminology is in
`D:\Email_Official\CONTEXT.md`; the correspondence and PO are in `BRB_MED_I_Emails/`.

**The standoff (not recorded in any file):** the Chairman will not release funds until his
land acquisition closes; Med I will not issue the Proforma Invoice or start work until funds
move. His PO requested the PI back in March, so by the plain wording of his own document the
ball has sat in Med I's court since then. He has never chased it — but it is Med I's main
exposure if the delay is ever raised, and no email should press him on the L/C without
addressing it.

**Strategy chosen 31 July 2026** for the follow-up email after the 25 July call: lead with the
embedded items that must be cast into the bunker concrete (his next physical action, and the
one argument that is technical rather than commercial), then use his own stated preference for
paying in small stages to justify amending Clause 06 from no-partial-shipment to partial
shipment — framed as complying with him, never as a relaxation Med I is seeking. The ask is
the smallest first instalment that opens Med I's back-to-back L/C to SHI, deliberately not
SHI's 40% advance, which he has already refused.

**Blocked on:** the first-instalment figure. The draft at
`BRB_MED_I_Emails/DRAFT_2026-07-29_PI_and_Timeline.md` carries `[___]` placeholders in both
language versions and must not be sent until they are filled.

**Deferred deliberately:** partial shipment adds freight cost. The draft is silent on it
rather than promising the restructure is free; that conversation is expected to land with the
revised PI. Also unresolved — the PO says model **CYPRIS HM-12** while the May site-visit email
says **HM-12S**, which will cause a discrepancy at document presentation under an L/C.
